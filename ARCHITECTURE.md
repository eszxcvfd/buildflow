# Architecture Entry Point

The technical platform and architecture of BuildFlow are governed by accepted **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)** and foundation documentation.

The old scaffold architecture is not authoritative for the rebuilt project.

## Canonical Architecture Authority

1. **Architecture Decision Records**: [docs/architecture/adr/README.md](docs/architecture/adr/README.md) (`ADR-001` through `ADR-015`)
2. **System Context**: [docs/foundation/SYSTEM-CONTEXT.md](docs/foundation/SYSTEM-CONTEXT.md)
3. **Module Map**: [docs/foundation/MODULE-MAP.md](docs/foundation/MODULE-MAP.md)
4. **Data Baseline**: [docs/foundation/DATA-BASELINE.md](docs/foundation/DATA-BASELINE.md)
5. **Security Baseline**: [docs/foundation/SECURITY-BASELINE.md](docs/foundation/SECURITY-BASELINE.md)
6. **Definition of Ready & Done**: [docs/foundation/DEFINITION-OF-READY-DONE.md](docs/foundation/DEFINITION-OF-READY-DONE.md)

Detailed module designs are created just in time per module using [docs/foundation/templates/MODULE-DESIGN-TEMPLATE.md](docs/foundation/templates/MODULE-DESIGN-TEMPLATE.md) before implementation.
